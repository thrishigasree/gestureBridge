GestureBridge

GestureBridge removes communication barriers by translating sign language into meaningful text in real time, enabling inclusive communication across healthcare, education, public services, workplaces, and everyday interactions.

Live Translation Ecosystem

Features & Technical Highlights

Harnessing advanced client-side vision and NLP for seamless communication

AI Gesture Recognition

Real-time hand landmark tracking and gesture parsing via high-fidelity joint coordination meshes.

Real-Time Translation

Sub-100ms pipeline transforming hand coordinates into text tokens instantly as you sign.

Natural Language Generation

Converting disconnected gesture vectors into grammatically correct and fluid English sentences.

Browser-Based

Run full spatial AI calculations directly in the browser. Zero software installations required.

No Wearable Hardware

Works with a basic built-in laptop camera or web camera. No expensive haptic gloves or trackers.

Accessible Anywhere

Built for mobile, tablet, and desktop viewports, optimizing inclusive access in any environment.

Expanding the Horizons of Communication

GestureBridge aims to become an ubiquitous translation engine embedded in everyday digital networks and physical kiosks.

Mobile App Integration

Custom native iOS & Android applications with lightweight on-device neural nets, running sign translation locally without active cellular connection.

Q4 2026

Healthcare Kiosks

Assisting clinical environments and emergency rooms by enabling medical staff to immediately comprehend deaf patients' signs when human translators are unavailable.

Clinical Trials

Public Service Kiosks

Embedding translation cameras in ticket stations, airport check-ins, and information helpdesks to offer accessibility assistance in public nodes.

Government R&D

Video Conferencing API

SDK and browser extensions for Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams to auto-transcribe deaf presenters directly into caption lines in real-time.

Beta Phase

Multilingual Support

Bridging signs directly to multi-language transcripts (e.g. translating American Sign Language to French, Spanish, or Hindi sentences).

In Development

B2B Customer Support

Enabling retail assistants, banking tellers, and hotels to integrate translation portals inside their standard POS systems to accommodate all client requirements.
